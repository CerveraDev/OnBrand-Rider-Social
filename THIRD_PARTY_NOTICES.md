# Third-Party Research Notices

OnBrand Design's copy-quality guidance is an original, project-specific synthesis informed by the following public resources:

- Peter Yang, [No AI Slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop), MIT License.
- Jalaaldeen, [Anti-AI-Slop Writing](https://github.com/jalaalrd/anti-ai-slop-writing), MIT License.
- WikiProject AI Cleanup, [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), used as a descriptive field guide with its stated caveats.

These external skills are not bundled, installed, or invoked by OnBrand Design. Their strict word bans, structural quotas, and automatic-invocation behavior are not adopted wholesale. OnBrand Design uses its own contextual editorial rules, with factual accuracy and approved project voice taking priority.
